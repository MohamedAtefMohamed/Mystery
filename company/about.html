OUR MISSION & PHILOSOPHY

Real-World Threat Actors for Your Defense

MYSTERY was founded on a simple principle: compliance checklists don't stop determined attackers. Only researchers who actively break software can effectively defend it.

Research-Driven Approach

Our security researchers spend their time dissecting zero-day vulnerabilities, building offensive security tools, and reporting high-severity security flaws to major software vendors.

When you work with MYSTERY, that exact research mindset is applied directly to your web apps, mobile clients, APIs, and internal networks.

Why We Reject Scanners

Automated tools test static patterns. They cannot understand multi-step business logic, privilege escalation chains, or state-machine vulnerabilities.

Every finding reported by MYSTERY is manually identified, validated with a clean Proof of Concept (PoC), and triaged to guarantee zero false positives.
