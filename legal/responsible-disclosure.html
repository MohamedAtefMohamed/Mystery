Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

At MYSTERY, security is paramount. We appreciate independent security researchers who test our systems in accordance with this disclosure policy.

Safe Harbor Guidelines

If you act in good faith to disclose security vulnerabilities to us, we will consider your research authorized, work with you to understand and resolve the issue, and not take legal action against you.

Reporting Process

Send details of your findings to mystery.security@gmail.com. Please encrypt sensitive technical details using our PGP key where appropriate.
